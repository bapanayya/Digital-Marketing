## 🎯 Executive Overview
This portal provides a state-of-the-art, responsive, and interactive educational learning environment for undergraduate degree students undertaking the **Digital Marketing Skill Enhancement Course** under the Andhra Pradesh State Council of Higher Education (APSCHE) curriculum.

The web application features an academic dark/light theme, interactive algorithmic sandboxes, comprehensive chapter modules, question banks with model answers, and interactive certification mock exams.

---

## 🧭 Curricular Architecture & Tracks

### 📌 Track 1: Unit I &mdash; Foundations of Digital Marketing
- **Concept & POEM Model:** Paid, Owned, and Earned Media dynamics.
- **Traditional vs. Digital:** Evolution from 4Ps (Product, Price, Place, Promotion) to customer-centric 4Cs (Customer Solution, Cost to Satisfy, Convenience, Communication).
- **The 9 Pillars:** Website, SEM, Display Ads, Email, Blogs, SMM, Audio/Video/Interactive, Online PR, and Mobile Marketing.
- **Direct Channels:** In-depth Email marketing (segmentation, drip sequences, CAN-SPAM) and Mobile marketing (WhatsApp Business API, SMS, Geofencing).
- **Emerging Trends:** Generative AI & LLMs, Voice Search Optimization (VSO), Programmatic Advertising (RTB), and Cookieless first-party privacy.

### 🌐 Track 2: Unit II &mdash; Website Design, WordPress & SEO
- **Web Fundamentals:** Client-Server architecture, Domain Name Systems (DNS), Web Hosting tiers, and CMS comparison.
- **Elements of a Good Website:** Visual hierarchy, F/Z reading patterns, trust badges, and Mobile-First Responsive Web Design.
- **WordPress Mastery:** Posts vs Pages, Gutenberg modular block editing, and core plugins (Yoast SEO, WPForms, Wordfence Security, WP Super Cache).
- **UI/UX Principles:** Wireframing, information architecture, and Jakob Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics.
- **SEO Lifecycle:** Search engine mechanics (Crawling &rarr; Indexing &rarr; Ranking), Keyword Research & Search Intent (Informational, Navigational, Commercial, Transactional).
- **On-Page & Off-Page SEO:** HTML tags (Title, Meta Description, H1-H6, Alt, Canonical, Schema), Inbound link equity, PageRank, and Domain Authority.
- **Display Advertising & CRO:** Google Display Network (GDN) formats (Leaderboard, Medium Rectangle, Skyscraper), Landing Page 1:1 Attention Ratio, and A/B Split Testing.

### 📊 Track 3: Unit III &mdash; Social Media Marketing & Measurement
- **Social Media Ecosystem:** The Social Graph, algorithmic feed distribution, consumer behavior, and the Paid+Organic hybrid imperative.
- **Platform Deep Dives:** Facebook (Meta Ads Manager 3-tier hierarchy), LinkedIn (B2B lead generation & personal branding), Blogging (Hub-and-spoke topic clusters), Twitter/X (Real-time PR & Crisis management), YouTube (Video SEO & Audience Retention).
- **Customer Lifecycle:** Inbound Flywheel (Attract &rarr; Engage &rarr; Delight &rarr; Promote).
- **Quantitative Ad Metrics:** Mathematical formulas for Reach, Impressions, CTR, CPC, CPM, CPA/CAC, ROAS, and net ROI.
- **Web Telemetry & Attribution:** Sessions, GA4 Engagement Rate vs. Bounce Rate, and Multi-Touch Attribution models (First-Click, Last-Click, Linear, Time-Decay, Data-Driven).

### 🧪 Track 4: Curricular Activities & Practical Labs (Labs 1 &ndash; 5)
1. **Practical 1:** Digital Marketing Frameworks Critique (RACE & 4Cs applied to real brands).
2. **Practical 2:** End-to-End WordPress Blog Setup & Production Deployment.
3. **Practical 3:** Comprehensive 25-Point On-Page and Technical SEO Website Audit.
4. **Practical 4:** 5-Day Facebook Ads Campaign Execution, Split-Testing & Post-Campaign Report.
5. **Practical 5:** Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Implementation, Event Tracking & Executive Dashboard Reporting.

### 🎓 Track 5: Exams, Question Bank & Mock Tests
- **APSCHE University Question Bank:** Section A (Short Answer Questions with model points) and Section B (Long Essay Outlines).
- **Viva-Voce & Interview Preparation:** 30 rapid-fire questions covering WordPress, SEO, SEM, Meta Ads, and GA4 telemetry.
- **Unit 1, 2, and 3 Online Practice Tests:** Timed MCQs with instant verification and educational rationale.
- **Grand Semester Comprehensive Exam:** 50-question mock test covering the entire curriculum.

## 🛠️ Interactive Simulators & Sandboxes
1. **Google SERP & On-Page SEO Checker (`js/seo_simulator.js`):** Live desktop/mobile SERP preview, title pixel meter, meta description length validator, and real-time SEO health score.
2. **Digital Ad Spend & ROAS Calculator (`js/ad_roi_calculator.js`):** Dynamic sliders for budget, impressions, CTR, conversion rates, and order values computing CPC, CPM, CPA, ROAS, and net ROI with industry presets.
3. **Social Media Creative Studio (`js/social_simulator.js`):** Multi-platform post and sponsored ad creative mockup for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Twitter/X with simulated engagement rates.
4. **WordPress CMS Architecture Sandbox (`js/wordpress_simulator.js`):** Interactive WordPress dashboard simulating Posts vs Pages and toggling essential plugins (Yoast, WPForms, Wordfence, Cache) with real-time site health metrics.
5. **Inbound Funnel & Attribution Explorer (`js/analytics_funnel.js`):** 5-stage conversion funnel with interactive attribution model comparison (First-Touch, Last-Touch, Linear, Time-Decay, Data-Driven).
